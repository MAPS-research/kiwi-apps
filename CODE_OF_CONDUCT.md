# Code of Conduct

We welcome people of all backgrounds and experience levels. Help others participate by being respectful, specific, and patient.

- Discuss ideas and behavior without personal attacks. Give constructive feedback and accept corrections.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, impersonate others, or share private information without permission.
- Respect people's time and boundaries. Avoid spam, repeated unwanted contact, and misleading claims about an app or its authors.
- Keep public issues and pull requests focused on the directory and its contribution process.

These expectations apply in this repository's issues, pull requests, and other community spaces managed by its maintainers.

Report concerns to the repository maintainers through a private contact method they make available on their profiles. If no private route is listed, ask a maintainer for one without posting incident details. Do not publish sensitive reports or personal information in an issue. Share only the information needed to understand and address the concern.

Maintainers will consider the context and aim for a proportionate response. They may ask for a change in behavior, edit or remove content, close a discussion, or restrict participation. Maintainers are also expected to follow this code; if a concern involves one maintainer, contact another when available.
